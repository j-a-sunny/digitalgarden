**Definition & Purpose** Strategic planning is a long-term planning process through which organizations determine their mission, vision, long-term objectives, strategies, and resource allocation. Ultimately, strategic planning helps organizations adapt to environmental changes and achieve sustainable growth.

**Importance of Strategic Planning**
There are seven key reasons why this process is critical for an organization:

- **Provides Long-Term Direction:** Strategic planning establishes future organizational direction.
- **Helps Achieve Organizational Goals:** It aligns resources with objectives.
- **Improves Competitive Position:** Organizations can compete effectively.
- **Reduces Risk and Uncertainty:** Future threats and opportunities are anticipated.
- **Encourages Better Decision-Making:** Decisions become more systematic and logical.
- **Facilitates Coordination:** Departments work together efficiently.
- **Improves Organizational Performance:** Productivity and efficiency increase.

**Steps in Setting Strategic Plans**

To create an effective strategic plan, managers follow a six-step process:
- **Step 1: Define Mission and Vision.** The mission defines the organizational purpose, while the vision defines future aspirations.
- **Step 2: Environmental Scanning.** Organizations analyze their internal environment to identify strengths and weaknesses, and their external environment to identify opportunities and threats, collectively known as a SWOT Analysis.
- **Step 3: Setting Objectives.** Objectives should be SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.
- **Step 4: Formulation of Strategies.** Strategies are developed to achieve objectives.
- **Step 5: Implementation.** This phase requires the allocation of resources, employee involvement, and specific action programs.
- **Step 6: Evaluation and Control.** The final step is to monitor performance, compare results with standards, and take corrective actions.
